*Principles of Mechanics: Fundamental University Physics* is an open-access
textbook by **Salma Alrasheed** (King Abdullah University of Science and
Technology), published by Springer in 2019. It covers the core of a
university mechanics course: units and vectors, kinematics, Newton's laws,
work and energy, impulse and momentum, systems of particles, rotation of
rigid bodies, rolling and static equilibrium, central force motion, and
oscillatory motion — with worked examples and end-of-chapter problems
throughout.

This is a faithful MyST Markdown conversion of the printed book: all
equations are live LaTeX, all figures are the original book artwork, and
worked examples and problems are interactive directives.
